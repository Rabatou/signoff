# SignOff: go-to-market

## Beachhead customer
Small import businesses in **Dakar, Senegal** (5 to 20 employees) that pay foreign suppliers every month, mainly in China, Nigeria and Turkey. The owner acts as CFO, approves every payment personally, and is exposed to invoice fraud.

Why them first:
- A recurring, painful, measurable job: paying suppliers abroad.
- One decision maker (the owner), so sales cycles are short.
- Dense communities. Importers cluster in the same markets and associations, so word of mouth works.

## Value proposition
"Delegate supplier payments to an agent without losing control."
- **Time:** invoices checked and paid in minutes, not evenings.
- **Money:** stablecoin settlement on XRPL instead of costly, slow international transfers.
- **Safety:** fraud and duplicate detection, plus hard limits the agent can't bypass.

## Channels
1. **Chartered accountants and bookkeeping firms** (cabinets d'expertise comptable). Each one serves dozens of SMEs and is already trusted with their finances. Partner model: the firm onboards its clients and gets a revenue share.
2. **Importers' and traders' associations**, plus chambers of commerce: demos at member meetings.
3. **WhatsApp-first onboarding.** Owners already receive invoices on WhatsApp, so invoice capture by photo is next on the roadmap.

## Pricing (to validate in pilots)
- **Subscription** per business per month: the agent, the console and the fraud checks.
- **Small fee per payment**, kept clearly below what bank and money-transfer alternatives charge.
- Accountants get a share of the subscription revenue.

## Pilot plan (first 3 months)
1. 5 pilot importers through 1 partner accounting firm, on Testnet then a capped Mainnet pilot.
2. Measure: hours saved per month, fees saved, fraud and duplicates caught, payment time.
3. Turn the results into case studies, then go to the next firms.

## Expansion
- The rest of the WAEMU zone (same currency, same banking frictions), then Nigeria and Ghana.
- New corridors where African SMEs import (UAE, India).
- Supplier side: on-ramps and off-ramps with mobile-money partners, so suppliers can cash out locally.

## Key risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Regulation of stablecoin payments | Work with licensed on/off-ramp partners; stablecoin as settlement rail only. |
| Supplier acceptance | Start with suppliers already paid in USD; offer cash-out via partners. |
| Trust in AI | Ledger-enforced limits, full audit journal, and a pause button the owner controls. |
